Our Terms of Service

Last updated: October 27, 2026. By placing an order or using our website, you agree to the terms below.

1. Orders and Pricing

All prices are shown in Kenya Shillings (KES). An order is confirmed once we have accepted it and received your deposit or full payment. We may correct pricing errors and will tell you before going ahead with your order.

2. Payment

We accept M-Pesa, bank transfer and cash at our showroom. Ready-made items are paid in full before delivery. Custom furniture needs a 50% deposit to begin production, with the balance due on delivery.

3. Custom Orders

Custom pieces are made to the sizes, wood, fabric and finish you approve in your quotation. Because they are made specially for you, custom orders cannot be cancelled or returned once production has started. Natural wood and fabric dye lots can vary slightly from samples.

4. Delivery

We deliver within Nairobi and to other parts of Kenya for a fee that depends on the location. Delivery times given are estimates. Please make sure someone is available to receive the furniture and that the entrance and stairs are wide enough for the item.

5. Returns and Refunds

Ready-made items in their original condition can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund. Items that are damaged or not as described will be repaired, replaced or refunded at no cost to you. Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery if anything is wrong.

6. Warranty

We guarantee our wooden frames against defects in materials and workmanship for 10 years. The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, damage from misuse, water or pests, or changes made to the furniture by anyone other than Oakline.

7. Care of Your Furniture

To keep your furniture looking its best, keep it away from direct sunlight and damp, clean spills quickly and use the care instructions that come with your item.

8. Privacy

We handle your personal information as described in our Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to These Terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version on this page at the time of your order applies to that order.
