Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information Oakline Furniture collects when you shop with us, and how we keep it safe.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Information We Collect

When you browse our website, visit our showroom, place an order or contact us, we collect the information we need to serve you and deliver your furniture.

Personal Information

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your delivery address and directions to your home or office
  • Order details, such as the furniture, fabric, color and size you choose
  • Payment details, such as your M-Pesa number or transaction reference

Usage Data

We also collect basic information about how you use our website, such as the pages you view, the products you look at, your device type and your browser. This helps us improve the site and show you the furniture you care about.

How We Use Your Information

We only use your information for purposes connected to your furniture order and your experience with Oakline.

Primary Uses

  1. To process your orders, quotes and custom furniture requests
  2. To arrange delivery, assembly and after-sales support
  3. To answer your questions and keep you updated on your order
  4. To send offers and new collections, only if you have agreed to receive them

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We share your information only with partners who help us serve you, such as delivery drivers, payment providers and upholstery or workshop partners. They may use your details only to complete the task we give them. We may also share information where the law requires it.

We Never Sell Your Data

Your personal information is never sold or rented to advertisers or other companies.

Data Security

We take sensible steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorized access. Only team members who need your details to do their job can see them.

Encryption

Information sent through our website is protected with secure (HTTPS) connections.

Secure Storage

Customer records are stored in protected systems with restricted access.

Your Rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have control over your personal information. You can contact us at any time to use these rights.

Right to Access

Ask what personal information we hold about you and request a copy.

Right to Correction

Ask us to fix details that are wrong or out of date, such as your address or phone number.

Right to Deletion

Ask us to delete your information, unless we need to keep it for warranty or legal reasons.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies, which are small files stored on your device. They help the site work properly, show us which furniture is popular and make our offers more relevant. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the site may not work as well.

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Keep the site working, such as remembering items in your cart Session
Analytics Show us how visitors use the site so we can improve it 2 Years
Marketing Show relevant furniture offers on other websites 1 Year

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Please check back occasionally to see the latest version.
