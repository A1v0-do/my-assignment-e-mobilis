Service Details

Can't find the exact piece you have in mind? We'll design and build it for you, to fit your space, your style and your budget.

Custom Furniture

Furniture made to your measurements, in the wood, fabric and finish you choose, and built by hand in our Nairobi workshop.

Standard sizes don't always fit real homes. Whether you have a narrow hallway, an awkward corner or a large family that needs a longer dining table, we make pieces that fit your space exactly, so no room is wasted and nothing feels squeezed in.

You work directly with our designers and craftsmen from the first sketch to the final delivery. We show you samples of timber and fabric, agree on the design and price up front, and keep you updated while your furniture is being made.

What You Get

Made-to-Measure Sizing

Every piece is built to your exact length, width and height, so it fits your room perfectly.

Choice of Timber

Pick from durable hardwoods such as mahogany, oak and cypress, with stains and finishes to match your decor.

Fabrics & Colors

Choose from linen, velvet, leather and stain-resistant fabrics in dozens of colors. Swatches can be brought to your home.

Quality Guarantee

Every custom frame comes with a 10-year warranty, and we fix any workmanship issue free of charge.

How It Works

01

Consultation & Measuring

We visit your home or meet you at our workshop to discuss your needs, take measurements and understand your style.

02

Design & Quotation

We prepare a sketch of your piece, show you wood and fabric samples, and give you a clear quote before any work begins.

03

Crafting

Our craftsmen build the frame, shape the joints, upholster and finish your furniture by hand, with photo updates along the way.

04

Delivery & Setup

We deliver, place and assemble your furniture in your chosen room, and remove all packaging when we're done.

Service Information

  • Lead Time: 3 to 6 weeks
  • Deposit: 50% to start production
  • Includes: Design sketch, material samples, delivery and assembly
  • Pieces: Sofas, beds, dining sets, wardrobes, desks, shelves
