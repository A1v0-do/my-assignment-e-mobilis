About

About Us

Oakline began as a small family workshop. Today we design and build furniture that blends timeless style with everyday practicality.

Crafted With Care, Made to Be Lived In

We believe great furniture should feel as good as it looks, and stay that way for decades.

Our designers start with real homes and real routines. From a sofa deep enough for movie nights to a dining table that seats the whole family, each piece is shaped around how you actually live.

We source responsibly harvested hardwoods, durable fabrics and genuine leather, and we back every item with a long-term warranty so you can buy with confidence.
